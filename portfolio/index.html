Featured work

sol-exact-quote

An open-source library that returns the exact output of a swap on seven Solana DEX venues (Meteora DLMM & DAMM v2, Raydium CLMM/CP/v4, Orca Whirlpool, PumpSwap) from raw account bytes. It models the fee components most quoters miss — the Raydium CLMM dynamic fee, the Orca adaptive fee, the Raydium CP creator fee, PumpSwap's tiered fee — and it refuses rather than guessing when it cannot be sure. Every quote is checked against a live mainnet simulation: 125 of 125 exact to the unit.
